Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Q: Do I need a powerful computer to process the scans?
A: No. The scanner utilizes an edge-to-cloud architecture. Heavy photogrammetry processing is offloaded to elastic GPU instances in the cloud.
Q: How does the scanner know when to stop moving vertically?
A: The scanner features an adaptive loop powered by a Time-of-Flight (ToF) laser sensor. If two consecutive readings hit the back wall instead of the object, it terminates the scan early to save time.
Q: What file formats can I export?
A: You can export high-quality textured meshes (OBJ), 3D printable meshes (STL, 3MF), and web-optimized, Draco-compressed files (GLB).
Q: Why does the system delay the scan after I press start?
A: Low-cost cameras and LED lights suffer from thermal drift during warmup. The system waits for the internal temperature and luminous flux to stabilize (drift < 3% over 10s) before locking exposure.
Q: Can I scan transparent or highly reflective objects?
A: Passive photogrammetry naturally struggles with textureless surfaces. For highly reflective materials, applying a washable 3D scanning spray is recommended.
